How it works

byoa sits in your backend, between your users and their agents.

Your app calls agents.run(userId, task). byoa starts a sandbox, loads the user's setup, and runs the user's own harness on the user's own plan.

Safe by design

Real tokens never go into the sandbox.

The agent in the sandbox gets a short-lived job token. The proxy in your backend adds the real MCP token to each request.

Every job gets a fresh sandbox.

  2. 02Load the setup
  3. 03Run the job

BYOA IS

  • A library that runs inside your app's own backend
  • Open source, under the Apache 2.0 license
  • A way to store tokens in your own database
  • A runner for the real, unmodified claude and codex binaries

BYOA IS NOT

  • A hosted service. We run no server and never see your users' tokens
  • A way to share one user's AI login between apps
  • A modified client, or a client that pretends to be another
  • A UI kit. byoa gives data and events; your app draws its own UI
