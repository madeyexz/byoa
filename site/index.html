Who brings what

Your app brings the product.
Each user brings the agent.

You build the app and pick the sandbox. Each user brings their plan and their tools. byoa runs inside your backend and runs each job in a new sandbox.

The future of AI-native apps

AI is electricity.
A plan is a battery pack.

AI-native apps are the future. But an app cannot pay for each user's AI by usage: the numbers do not work. AI subscriptions solve this. We bet on a future where everyone has a $20 AI plan, or the same in another form, and that plan powers every app they use. In that future, every app is AI-native, so nobody says "AI-native app" anymore.

Battery packs come in different sizes, and one pack powers all the appliances in your home. This is the economy we bet on. We build byoa to make it come sooner, so that every developer can build on it.

Get started

Start in three steps.

Install byoa from npm, with npm or Bun. Then follow Getting started in the docs.

  • 1Install from npm Needs Node 22.18 or later, or Bun.
  • 2Write your first agent run Follow the getting started guide in the docs.
  • 3Run the to-do example An AI-native to-do list. Its backend uses byoa in 39 lines.
terminal
npm install @madeyexz/byoa
bun add @madeyexz/byoa
